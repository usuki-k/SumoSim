## Implementation Direction

Add small explicit arena-transform controls.

Recommended CLI:

```
--arena-offset-x PX
--arena-offset-y PX
--arena-scale SCALE
```

Master-space units for offsets.

Semantics MUST be documented clearly.

Recommended semantics:
- positive `arena-offset-x`: move painted arena content right
- positive `arena-offset-y`: move painted arena content down
- `arena-scale`: uniform scale around canvas center before translation

Do not introduce rotation or non-uniform scale.

### Why scale may be needed

The accepted source is nearly exactly 16:9.

A meaningful translation of a full-frame image can expose blank edges.

Therefore:
- permit a small uniform overscale/crop
- translate the scaled arena
- crop back to exact 3840×2160

The Tool MUST fail if requested scale/offset cannot cover the full output canvas.

Do not fill exposed areas with black, blur, clone, AI inpainting, or procedural content.

1. validate source aspect
2. create the existing exact 16:9 normalized representation
3. apply uniform scale about canvas center
